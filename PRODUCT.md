# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners in Belfort and the Nord Franche-Comté area (30-65), often researching on
a phone in the evening, comparing 2-4 builders before committing to works that
range from €15k insulation jobs to €300k+ custom homes. They are anxious about
budget overruns, abandoned job sites, and cowboy contractors. Bilingual
audience: French is the primary market language, English serves expat
homeowners in the region. The job to be done: understand what DESTPEC can do,
trust them, and request a quote (devis) with minimal friction.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for DESTPEC Bâtiment, a full-service construction and
renovation company. It exists to convert visitor anxiety into a quote request:
prove competence (réalisations, 10-year warranty, certifications), prove
reliability (fixed timelines, single point of contact), and make the devis
flow the easiest action on the site (5-step interactive flow with live
estimate and aid calculator). Success = qualified devis submissions and
callback bookings.

## Brand Personality

Master craftsman's workshop at dusk: solid, warm, precise, quietly confident.
Three words: grounded, exacting, warm. Voice in French: artisanal pride,
commitment ("notre parole de compagnon"). Voice in English: direct,
reassuring, no-surprises pragmatism. Not literal translation, tone shifts per
language.

## Anti-references

- The flat stacked-card layout of the provided reference mockup (use its
  colors/type/components DNA only, never its structure).
- Generic template-builder sites: identical icon cards, hero-metric SaaS
  blocks, stock-photo carousels with no point of view.
- Cream-everything "warm AI default" pages: the light sections are bone
  (#EFEAE1) used in alternation with near-black, never wall-to-wall beige.

## Design Principles

1. Cinematic, not stacked. Full-bleed imagery, off-grid overlaps, asymmetric
   grids, alternating dark/light rhythm. Each fold commits to one idea.
2. Show the work being built. The signature scroll build-sequence (foundation
   to roof) turns the services list into the act of construction itself.
3. Proof beats claims. Counting stats, filterable réalisations with
   before/after sliders, pulled-through reviews, warranty and aid badges sit
   next to every promise.
4. The devis flow is the product. Five steps, visual pickers, live estimate,
   aid calculator, photo upload. Zero dead ends, every step shows progress.
5. Graceful degradation. Every animation (build-sequence, parallax, tilt,
   magnetic buttons) has a static or instant fallback; reduced motion is
   respected everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 on both dark and light sections (bone
text on near-black, near-black text on bone; terracotta reserved for
large/bold elements or darkened to a passing shade for small text).
prefers-reduced-motion disables parallax, tilt, magnetic pull and the
build-sequence scrub (static assembled house). Focus states visible on all
interactive elements. Form steps navigable by keyboard, errors announced.
