## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Not AI slop.** Every listed ban was checked mechanically and by eye: no side-stripe borders (zero `border-left`/`border-right` >1px in styles.css), no gradient text (no `background-clip`), no glassmorphism (no `backdrop-filter`), no repeated eyebrow scaffolding (eyebrows appear exactly three times and each earns it), no text overflow at any breakpoint (`scrollWidth === clientWidth` on all pages, both breakpoints). The 01–05 numbering is legitimately earned: those numbers map to real construction stages that drive the SVG house build.

It escapes the saturated editorial-typographic lane on three counts: the palette is warm and chromatic with terracotta load-bearing rather than decorative; it commits to photography as content rather than texture; and the scroll-scrubbed SVG house is a bespoke mechanism no template ships.

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs` over `dist/` returned exit 2, 30 findings across 3 rules. `overused-font` (Montserrat) ×12 and `single-font` ×12 are **false positives** — verified via `document.fonts`, Playfair Display 400/500/600 all load and render (probe widths: Playfair 863px vs Georgia 837px vs generic serif 753px for the same string). `numbered-section-markers` ×6: the home-page hits are real (`.build-list__n` renders 01–05, legitimately); the devis/réalisations hits are false positives reading day/month fragments as section markers.

**The detector missed everything that actually matters.** The two P0 defects below were found by design review and confirmed by browser measurement, not by static scan.

## Overall Impression

This site is not badly designed. It is well designed and shipping broken. The copy is the best thing here: falsifiable, checkable commitments ("et si notre réponse est non, on vous dira pourquoi", "pénalités de retard à notre charge", "chaque étape masquée est photographiée avant d'être refermée") rather than adjectives. The aid calculator volunteers bad news. The scroll-driven house build is genuinely bespoke.

And the quote form — the thing the entire site funnels into — throws a TypeError on line 1 and does nothing. The single biggest opportunity is not aesthetic. It is that three of the site's four interactive proof mechanisms do not work, and for an audience whose defining fear is contractors who do not follow through, the site currently demonstrates the exact failure mode it is selling against.

## What's Working

**The scroll-driven build sequence.** Five hand-authored SVG stages scrubbed against the adjacent list's scroll position, each service highlighting as its stage draws. It works because it is not decoration: the content genuinely is an ordered sequence (you cannot roof before you frame), so the animation carries the same information as the list. Degrades correctly under reduced motion and at ≤860px.

**The aid calculator's honesty architecture.** `aidsEligible()` returns null for construction, extension and aménagement, and the panel says so plainly. Pick the highest income bracket on a renovation and it tells you MaPrimeRénov' is gone. A lead-generation form that volunteers bad news costs conversions short-term and is the most credible thing on the site.

**The copy's refusal to oversell.** Every promise is falsifiable and checkable. That is precisely the register that converts a homeowner who has been burned before. The design of the words is currently stronger than the design of the pixels.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The devis flow is dead on arrival
**What**: `main.js:251` does `root.parentElement.querySelector('[data-devis-config]')`. `root` is `.devis`, its parent is `.wrap`, but the config `<script>` is emitted outside `.wrap` as a sibling inside `section.devis-section` (`pages.js:642`). Lookup returns null, line 252 throws, and the entire devis IIFE never runs.

**Verified**: console shows `Uncaught TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'textContent')` on `/fr/devis/` and `/en/quote/` on every load. Zero `[data-progress-step].is-current`. `[data-days]` empty. `[data-submit]` permanently hidden. Clicking Continue produces a byte-for-byte identical DOM.

**Why it matters**: every path funnels here (10 CTAs on `/prestations` alone). 100% of quote submissions fail. And because the form has no `action` and no `method`, there is no no-JS fallback either, so the project's stated graceful-degradation principle does not hold on the one page that matters.

**Fix**: `document.querySelector('[data-devis-config]')`, wrap the IIFE in try/catch so config failure degrades to a plain visible form, give both forms a real action/method. Make `flow-test.mjs` assert against the built page.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P0] The before/after slider is draggable only in a 28px strip at the top
**What**: `.ba__range { height: 100% }` (specificity 0-1-0) is outranked by `input[type='range'] { height: 28px }` (0-1-1 — the attribute selector wins).

**Verified**: frame renders 352×236, the range input 352×**28**. `elementFromPoint` at the visible circular grip's centre returns `IMG.ba__img`, not the input. Only the top ~10px of the figure is draggable. Eight sliders affected.

**Why it matters**: "show the work being built" and "proof beats claims" are two of the five stated design principles, and the before/after comparison delivers both. The page instructs the user to drag. They drag the handle, nothing moves, and they conclude the site is broken.

**Fix**: `.ba .ba__range { height: 100% }`, or scope the generic range styling to `.devis input[type='range']`. Verify with `elementFromPoint` at the grip centre afterwards.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] The feedback system is invisible
Three compounding defects: (1) `.field-error { color: var(--terra-strong) }` (0-1-0) is outranked by `.section--bone p` (0-2-0), so error text computes to the same colour as surrounding body copy — measured identical, 8.62:1 both. For `type` and `consent` there is no input border to fall back on, so the error carries no visual signal at all. (2) After a failed Continue, `document.activeElement` is BODY; errors can sit a full screen above the tap point. (3) The final submit fails silently when no callback slot is picked: confirm stays hidden, zero visible errors, and the only feedback is written into a `clip-path: inset(50%)` element.

**Fix**: scope the error rule, add a non-colour cue, focus and scroll to the first invalid control, give step 5 a real visible error element.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P1] Mobile has no route to the quote for 8.8 screens
`styles.css:1168` hides the persistent header CTA at ≤860px, and the mobile nav panel exposes no phone number. Measured on `/fr/` at 375px: devis CTA at y=568, next devis CTA at y=**7711**, document height 10637. A 7143px gap covering services, stats, featured projects, method and reviews — every section whose job is to build the confidence that produces the click, with nowhere to put that confidence when it arrives.

Compounding on `/fr/devis/` at 375px: the live estimate — the flow's entire premise — is pushed to y=1738, below the form (607) and below the nav buttons (1595). And step labels are `display:none` below 640px, so the stepper is five unlabelled circles.

The stated target user is a homeowner on a phone in the evening. On that device the site hides its CTA, its phone number, its price and its progress.

**Fix**: persistent bottom bar on mobile with "Devis gratuit" + `tel:` after the hero scrolls out; move the estimate above the form at ≤1080px; replace hidden step labels with a visible "Étape 2 / 5 · Détails".

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Craft defects that read as unfinished
- **Hero sits 279px off the page grid.** `.hero` is `display:flex`, so `.hero__inner` shrink-to-fits: measured x=332 w=761 while every other `.wrap` is x=53 w=1320. The author already fixed this exact flex trap on `.page-head--media .page-head__inner` with `width: 100%`. Fix: same one-liner.
- **`undefined` printed in the income question.** `pages.js:432` emits `<small>${b.hint}</small>` unconditionally; the `unknown` bracket has no hint. Renders literally as "Je ne sais pas / non concerné**undefined**" at the most sensitive moment in the flow.
- **Four of seven flagship projects are ~400 km outside the stated service area.** The site promises "à moins de 30 km" and lists twelve Territoire-de-Belfort communes, while réalisations sit in Morigny-Champigny, Saint-Chéron, Étréchy and Boissy-le-Cutté (Essonne), with ids `morigny`, `dourdan`, `etampes`, `saintcheron`, `arpajon`. The proof section contradicts the pitch, and this audience checks maps.
- **Prices disagree between pages.** `/prestations` says 850–1500 €/m² for renovation; the estimator computes 600–950. Two different numbers from the same company, for an audience whose defining fear is budget surprises.
- **Main services list fails contrast.** `.build-list__item { opacity: 0.55 }` gives body copy 3.33:1 at 16px on desktop. Four of five descriptions of what the company does are below the floor at any scroll position.
- **Primary button text is 4.22:1** (bone on terracotta at 11.5–13.4px/600) across all pages — below AA for its size. The "Faire une autre demande" ghost button on the confirmation screen computes 1.00:1: bone on bone, invisible.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

## Minor Observations

- Réalisations filter group `aria-label` is `r.filters[0].label`, so screen readers announce the group as "Tous".
- No empty state for filters; two categories return a single project, a third would produce a blank page.
- All four certification cards render the identical `shield` icon — four glyphs carrying zero information.
- "97 % de clients qui nous recommandent" is the only unsourced claim on a site whose stated principle is "proof beats claims".
- Callback day radios use the localised display string as their value rather than the ISO date computed two lines earlier.
- Contact subject `<option>` elements have no `value` attribute.
- france-renov.gouv.fr is plain text, not a link — the one place a user could independently verify the aid claims.
- Phone displays unformatted on the confirmation recap.
- English page renders "97 %" with the French space convention.
- Preserved and correct: zero horizontal overflow at both breakpoints; type scale ratios all ≥1.25; hero clamp maxes at 5.2rem under the 6rem ceiling; letter-spacing bottoms at −0.02em inside the −0.04em floor; one `<h1>` per page; alt text present and correctly empty on aria-hidden images; `:has(input:focus-visible)` focus states on every custom control; thorough `prefers-reduced-motion` block.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the devis flow is "the product", why is it the only thing on the site with no automated check that it renders? There are four tooling scripts in the repo root and none caught a TypeError on line 1 of the conversion funnel.
2. Is a five-step form the right shape for a €300k decision, or is it a €50 checkout's shape applied to a €300k decision? What if step 1 offered a fourth door — "je préfère qu'on m'appelle" — capturing a name and number in eight seconds?
3. The aid calculator is honest enough to tell you you are not eligible. Why is the price not honest about its own uncertainty? A 4.5× range is presented with the visual authority of a quote while its caveat is 11.5px at 4.24:1.
4. The site's strongest asset is that Daniel Esteve is a real compagnon with 22 named people behind him, and there is not one human face anywhere in the interface. What changes if the confirmation ends with the photo and mobile number of the specific conducteur de travaux who will ring tomorrow at 8h30?
